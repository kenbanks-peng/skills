A calm visual direction for Fieldnotes, a fictional research notebook for people who read deeply and organize lightly.

Scope: visual foundations and component specimens · Audience: product and engineering

The design objective

Keep the writing surface quiet while making navigation and primary actions easy to find. This proposal prioritizes reading comfort over dashboard density.

03 / Components

One primary action at a time

Create noteBrowse library

Static visual specimens, not working controls. Production buttons need focus, hover, disabled, and loading states.

Use 8px control corners, 18px card corners, and a 24px layout gap. Keep secondary actions visually subordinate.
